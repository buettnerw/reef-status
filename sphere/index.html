Reef Planet

Every test since 19 October, grown into a little world. Healthy tests rise as glowing reef mountains above the sea; trouble sinks below it. Drag to turn, pinch to zoom, tap a mountain.

How to read it.
Mountain ranges — each test is one range, running from the south pole (19 October) to the north pole (today). Its base glows in the test kit's colour.
Height — the better the result, the higher the land. The glass sea sits at the "healthy" line: green reef peaks above the water are good; amber shores are drifting; red trenches sink below the sea when a test was out of range. Between two tests the land joins the readings smoothly.
Lights — each small light on a ridge is a real test you did (green, amber or red).
The ring of light is the day you have selected; the land north of it hasn't happened yet and lies dark under the sea. A beacon on each range shows that day's value; red beacons pulse and drip.
Pink rings — milestones: water changes, the brass valves, the PhosBond reactor, the nitrate rule, carbon, UV.
The glow around the planet and the score in the corner: the whole reef (70% average of all tests, 30% the worst one).
