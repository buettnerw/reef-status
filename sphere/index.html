How to read it.
Lines of longitude — each glowing line running pole to pole is one test, in its test kit's colour. Time runs upward: 19 October sits at the south pole, today at the north pole.
Beads — each dot on a line is one test result: green in target, amber drifting, red out. The better the result, the higher the bead floats above the glass.
The crown — the cyan ring is the day you have selected. Spikes stand up from it, one per test: a tall green spike is a healthy reading. Short red spikes pulse and drip. A frosted white spike means that test is overdue.
Pink rings — milestones: the brass valves, water changes, the PhosBond reactor, the nitrate rule, UV lights.
The polyp in the middle scores the whole reef (70% average of all tests, 30% the worst one). Tentacles out and swaying when it is happy, pulled in and twitching when stressed.
Story replays every day since 19 October: watch the crown climb the globe.
