Reef Health Orb

Each glass tube is one test, filled with the colour of its test kit. The coral polyp in the middle opens when the reef is happy.

How to read it. A tube is full when that test sits inside its target. It drains as the value drifts towards the warning limits and is empty past them. The glass rim glows green, amber or red; red tubes crack and leak. A frosted tube means the test is overdue, so trust it less.
The polyp scores the whole reef: 70% the average of all tubes (temperature, ammonia, nitrate, phosphate and alkalinity count extra), 30% the worst tube, because one bad parameter can hurt a reef on its own. High score: tentacles fully out and swaying. Low score: pulled in and twitching.
Story replays every test since 19 October.
